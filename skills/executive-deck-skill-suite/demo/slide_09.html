Key Risks & Mitigation

Runtime Reliability

HIGH

Scaling from 4K to 50K daily workloads risks p95 latency breaches and cascading failures.

Mitigation

Chaos testing, per-tenant isolation, and a 400ms SLO with automated rollback.

Pricing Pressure

HIGH

Hyperscaler bundles could compress per-seat pricing by 20-30% in renewal cycles.

Mitigation

Shift revenue mix toward usage-based workloads and marketplace take-rate.

Regulatory Timing

MEDIUM

EU AI Act control deadlines may land before our Q3 GA date.

Mitigation

Dedicated compliance squad with external counsel review at every milestone.

Talent Concentration

MEDIUM

Deep runtime knowledge sits with six engineers; attrition is a single point of failure.

Mitigation

Pair-rotation program, documentation sprints, and two senior backfills in Q1.
